## Firmware and GateWare

The camera video [can be displayed on the screen]
(https://github.com/raspberrypi/rpicam-apps/blob/main/apps/rpicam_hello.cpp)
and the screen can actually be a 24 bit + controls [Display Parallel
Interface]
(https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/raspberry-pi.html#display-parallel-interface-dpi)
driving an FPGA.  At 60Mhz, the display would be 1 MegaPixel. Maybe as
much as a 2 MegaPixel display is possible.
